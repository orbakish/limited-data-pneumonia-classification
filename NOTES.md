# Project Notes

A shared running log of open questions, decisions, and ideas — so nothing gets lost between
work sessions or between us.

**How to use this file:**
- **Open questions** — anything that needs a decision or an answer from the other person.
  Note who it's for and its status.
- **Decisions made** — once resolved, move it here *with the reasoning*, so we don't
  re-litigate it later or forget why we chose it.
- **Parking lot** — ideas worth remembering but deliberately not doing now.

Question numbers are stable IDs: once a question is answered it moves to Decisions, and its
number is never reused. Gaps in the numbering are normal.

Add to it freely as things come up rather than waiting until the end of a session.

| **Transfer learning by fine-tuning (question #6).** Default: **full fine-tuning** - every layer trains. | **Training recipe (question #18)**, identical for all three architectures and all 27 runs: AdamW with weight decay 1e-4; learning rate split into 1e-3 for the new head and 1e-4 for the pretrained backbone; constant schedule; batch size 32; at most 30 epochs with early stopping after 5 epochs without improvement; early stopping and the saved checkpoint follow **validation ROC-AUC**; validation loss logged every epoch. | The new head starts random while the backbone is already good, so a split rate lets the head learn quickly without its early, noisy gradients overwriting pretrained features. A constant rate gives every run identical optimisation behaviour, whereas plateau schedules react to each run's validation noise. ROC-AUC is threshold-free; validation loss can rise from overconfidence while separation still improves. **Caveats:** (1) the dataset is known to be easy, so ROC-AUC may saturate near 0.98-0.99 and leave little headroom - the main signal may then be Grad-CAM stability; (2) a 20% epoch is about 28 updates against about 139 at 100%, so patience in epochs is more generous at larger sizes - the tuning phase checks that 20% runs stop before the cap and larger runs do not stop prematurely; (3) "identical recipe" does not mean identical architectures: MobileNetV3-Small and EfficientNet-B0 have 0.2 dropout in their heads (ResNet18 has none) and EfficientNet-B0 uses stochastic depth; (4) whether cuDNN runs deterministically on the GPU is decided explicitly in `src/train.py`. | 2026-09-15 |

All on `train_20`, no augmentation, seed 42, judged on the validation set only - the test set stays untouched. Fine-tuning depth (T04-T06) is judged by validation ROC-AUC with balanced accuracy as tiebreak; class weighting (T07-T09) by **balanced accuracy**, with ROC-AUC secondary.
